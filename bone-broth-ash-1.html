chicken soup 1

serves 5~ 1 hour it'll get better on day 3
for returning

ingredients

the base
  • chicken thighs4
  • bone broth4 cups
  • canned lentils, drained1 cup
  • sweet potato, japanese and purple, cubed small1
  • carrots1 cup
  • canned diced tomatoes with green chilies1 cup
  • tomato paste2 tbsp
  • yellow onion, diced1
  • fresh ginger, grated1 tbsp
  • coconut milk½ cup
  • fresh spinach2 cups
  • lion's mane mushroom, sliced thick1 cup
  • lemon, juiced½
  • olive or coconut oil3 tbsp
  • bay leaves2
  • salt1 tsp
the spice paste
  • cumin seed1 tsp
  • coriander seed1 tsp
  • black pepper½ tsp
  • cinnamon½ tsp
  • tears½ tsp
to serve
  • cooked rice2 cups
  • dried parsley or chives1 tsp

method

step 01
sear the chicken

pat chicken thighs dry — heat oil in your heaviest pot on medium-high until shimmering. add chicken and don't touch it for 4 minutes. flip, 4 more minutes. pull them out and set aside.

step 02
bloom the whole spices

lower heat to medium. in the same pot, add cumin seed and coriander seed. toast for 60-90 seconds, stirring constantly, until they pop

step 03
build the spice paste

add a splash more oil if needed. add onion and cook 8-10 minute. add ginger and stir for 2 minutes. add all the ground spices. stir constantly for 90 seconds. add tomato paste and smash it into the spices. cook 2 full minutes

step 04
broth

pour in bone broth and scrape everything off the bottom. add diced tomatoes, lentils, carrots, sweet potato, bay leaves, and salt. return the chicken thighs to the pot. bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer, lid on, 25 minutes

step 05
sear the lion's mane

slice lion's mane into thick slabs. heat a separate dry pan on medium-high and sear

step 06
shred and finish

pull chicken out and shred with two forks. return shredded chicken and lion's mane to the pot. pour in coconut milk. add spinach, lid on for 2 minutes. squeeze in lemon. remove bay leaves. taste and adjust salt
